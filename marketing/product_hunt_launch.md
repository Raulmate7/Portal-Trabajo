## 📝 Descripciones para la Ficha

### Descripción Corta (Máx 260 caracteres)
> The ultimate tech job aggregator in Spain. We parse thousands of IT vacancies every 6 hours by stack, city, and seniority. Includes dynamic salary calculators, custom email alerts, and specialized Telegram channels. No duplicates, just real data.

### Descripción Larga
> **Portal Trabajo IT** resolves the fragmentation of the technical job market in Spain by aggregating, deduplicating, and enriching job offers in real-time.
> 
> Key features:
> - **Continuous Aggregation**: Thousands of active jobs crawled every 6 hours.
> - **Granular Categorization**: Filter by exact stack (React, Node, Go, Rust, AWS, etc.), cities (including remote-first), and seniority (Junior, Mid, Senior).
> - **Salary Transparency**: Live calculator showing minimum, maximum, average, and median salaries computed from visible database data.
> - **Specialized Alerts**: Get instant emails for featured jobs matching your profile, and join dedicated Telegram channels by stack.

## 💬 Maker's Comment (Primer Comentario del Creador)

Este comentario se publica inmediatamente al lanzar el producto para contar la historia detrás del proyecto y fomentar la interacción de la comunidad.

```markdown
Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I'm thrilled to share Portal Trabajo IT with you today.

Finding a developer job in Spain can be incredibly frustrating. The market is fragmented across dozens of portals, descriptions are cluttered, and salary transparency is almost non-existent. Most platforms mix IT roles with non-tech jobs, and duplicate listings are everywhere.

We wanted to fix this by building a developer-first platform. Portal Trabajo IT scrapes and consolidates tech jobs in Spain every 6 hours. Our pipeline cleans duplicate entries, categorizes roles dynamically by technology and experience level, and extracts salary ranges to show real-time market stats.

What we've built:
1. 🔍 Focus: Strictly software development, DevOps, data, cloud, and mobile.
2. 🧮 Salary Engine: Dynamic statistics showing percentiles (P25, P50, P75) by tech, city, and seniority.
3. ⚡ Multi-channel Notifications: Onboarding alerts, personalized newsletters, and dedicated Telegram channels.
4. 🇪🇸/🇬🇧 Multi-language support for international developers looking to relocate or work remote from Spain.

We are actively improving our scrapers and adding more features. We'd love to hear your feedback, thoughts on the UI, and features you'd like to see next!

Thank you for your support! 🚀
```
